# Optional report-to-SPS draft handoff

Use only after a report recommendation is available and the user chooses a topic to develop. Keep the report and its evidence intact. This handoff creates a draft; it does not publish, schedule, submit for approval, edit SPS prompts or alter the report.

## Resolve the destination and production brief

Use the selected recommendation, source links, confirmed business profile and applicable workspace instructions to prepare a *proposed answer*, rather than asking the user to fill in a blank form. Show one compact brief with:

- the exact SPS workspace/agent connection, Page, pillar and platform/post format; identify any mapping from a report topic or programme to an SPS pillar as a proposal, not a fact;
- the current-year Hong Kong reader need and story thesis, why the recurring trigger applies now, the independent research questions and sources still needed;
- a concrete writing outline, intended visual evidence and its rights path, and any Page-required length, voice, CTA and hashtags;
- draft-only status, with any publication timing stated as an editorial target rather than an SPS schedule.

Ask a confirmation question such as: “I propose this workspace, Page, pillar, angle and outline. Is this right? You can approve it, correct any line, or leave this topic as a report recommendation.” Group dependent decisions into one reviewable proposal. Reuse known answers and offer a recommended choice for unresolved details; do not repeat questions whose answers are already in the report, Page settings or conversation. If the user corrects a field, revise the brief and confirm the affected decisions, then continue. Ask a narrow follow-up only when a material decision or required evidence remains unresolved. Do not interpret silence as agreement.

The user may provide source material, named contacts or a usable photo. Unless they limit research, independently search current sources to fill factual gaps; explain what a user-supplied item would add rather than requiring them to provide one. Ask about a missing interview, private fact or rights grant only when it cannot be independently established. Confirm the intended post format and the Page/pillar placement before writing to SPS.

## Research and eligibility before writing

The historical focal or competitor post is performance and topic evidence, not a source to copy. Research the current-year trigger, facts, dates and substantive Hong Kong relationship afresh. Record which claims each independent source supports and resolve discrepancies. A repeated seasonal topic may retain the same subject; write a new angle, structure, wording and visual treatment for the chosen Page. Do not reuse a competitor's copy, creative or photograph without a separately verified right to do so.

Read the target Page's *current* SPS governance, pillar and content-type rules and record their versions. Apply its factual, language, privacy, visual and rights gates before candidate completion. Where the active policy requires a directly matching usable real photograph, verify the actual image and rights; if none qualifies, hold or skip the whole post instead of creating a text-only or substitute-image draft. Treat the report's proposed hook or visual as a hypothesis until current research supports it.

Inspect the live SPS queue for duplicates and existing drafts of the same story. If one exists, propose continuing that specific draft and confirm this choice before editing it; do not create a duplicate. Verify the intended SPS connection's live agent identity, team/workspace and Page before any production read or write. Never use a different workspace's credentials as a fallback. If the requested destination or draft operation is unavailable, report the exact blocker and retain the approved brief locally; do not write to a different Page.

## Draft and verify

Once the user has confirmed the necessary destination and editorial choices and the story passes the active gates, prepare the full original caption, headline, source packet and eligible media under the Page's current rules. Present any remaining consequential editorial choice as a specific proposed answer for confirmation. Avoid serial approval questions for routine wording decisions already covered by the brief.

Create or update **one** SPS post in the confirmed workspace and Page, using the approved pillar and post format. Keep it Draft / Not Submitted; do not create a publish job or schedule. Read back the saved post ID, status, caption and media assignment, check that they match the approved brief, and report any remaining limits or rights holds. Preserve source and verification receipts without exposing credentials or private data.
