## Advertising review without a dedicated tag

Judge each post using the export, supplied context and accessible original post/media. Maintain a run ledger with post ID, classification, specific evidence, confidence and exclusion reason:

1. Confirmed ad: disclosed paid partnership/sponsorship or supplied reliable commercial status. Exclude.
2. Inferred commercial ad: the combined evidence clearly establishes a commissioned promotional purpose, such as explicit advertiser/campaign identity together with product-only sales messaging, campaign redemption or a sponsored promotional offer. Exclude under the user-authorised judgment rule and distinguish inferred from confirmed status.
3. Uncertain: commercial references without sufficient evidence. Retain with an uncertainty flag and check whether conclusions change when these are removed; unresolved material sensitivity limits the finding.
4. Editorial: independent news, parent-service guidance or critical/comparative product coverage. Retain.

Brand mentions, product images, outbound links, offers, giveaways or high interaction alone do not establish paid advertising. Assess their combined purpose and disclosure. Paid reach/boosting status remains unknown unless directly evidenced, even for an editorial-looking post. Apply the same commercial-evidence standard across approved competitors. Record confirmed/inferred exclusions and uncertain counts per page; show details in Methodology, not repetitive defensive case prose.

Reuse this brief for later Sunday Kiss runs and ask only for changed pages, labels, objectives or missing evidence. The default requested history is at least 12 completed months. Local one-off incidents, celebrity scandals and unique viral events stay in current-period diagnosis; seasonal parent needs, school/application cycles, repeatable holidays and predictable service deadlines can qualify for next-month historical planning after current-year verification.
