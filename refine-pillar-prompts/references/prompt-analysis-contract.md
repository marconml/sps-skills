# Prompt Analysis Contract

Read this reference after the refinement brief and prompt snapshot are confirmed.

## Prepare evidence

Use the same dates and timezone across compared pages. Preserve post ID/permalink, page, full caption, actual media, publication time, pillar/territory, format, selected KPI, supporting metrics, promotion status, and coverage flags. Missing values remain missing.

Exclude identified ads from organic comparisons using an authoritative field or approved rule. Retain collected, excluded, and analyzed counts. Unknown promotion status remains unknown.

Resolve exact or materially identical reposts before forming creative contrasts. Count each publication at page level, but treat unchanged caption-and-media executions as one creative. Different results for unchanged creative indicate a distribution or context confound, not a creative driver.

## Build comparable sets before revealing performance

Within the closest approved pillar, retrieve semantically similar posts and fix pairs or small clusters before revealing performance. Include high, middle, and low outcomes when the material permits. Inspect full captions, actual media, and publication times after retrieval, then test the proposed mechanism against weak performers and counterexamples.

If fewer than two posts are genuinely comparable, record insufficient evidence.

## Benchmarking

Compare the selected KPI, supporting metrics, topic treatment, hook, information order, visual thesis, format, timing, CTA, and known distribution controls.

- High performers: identify the likely success mechanism and what should be retained or scaled; do not force a rewrite.
- Low performers: identify the most plausible drag and propose a narrow improvement test.
- Middle cases and counterexamples: test whether the proposed mechanism survives beyond winners.

State supported explanations as Inferences, not causation.

## Reframing

Create an isolated review packet containing audience/pillar context, captions, media, and relevant current prompts. Hide performance, ranking/order, and Benchmarking conclusions. Review clarity, credibility, information order, visual understanding, and caption-image coordination. Every output remains a Hypothesis / Test until performance evidence supports it.

## Internalizing

Use only user-supplied or explicitly approved competitor examples. Validate column and metric meanings, map examples to relevant pillars or territories, and inspect captions and images together when media exists.

Extract abstract choices in story angle, information order, specificity, visual evidence, caption-image coordination, and CTA. Never copy wording, assets, layout, tagline, or source identity. Normalize performance within the same source and period where possible; public counts are directional signals, not reach or an engagement rate.

Actively search approved evidence for reliable same-event, person, study, source article, or highly similar topic cases across pages. Record the shared entities and incident details before revealing performance. Broad themes are not a same-topic match.

## Competitor discovery

Review material outside the current pillars. Group recurring territories by meaning, then show organic post count, selected-KPI distribution, period, contributing pages, and counterexamples. Recommend an opportunity only when sustained output and a performance signal support it; a few winners do not establish a new pillar.

## Corresponding

Use de-identified comments from the page's own posts. Separate audience comments, Page-authored replies, intentional CTA triggers, spam, and discussion about the underlying subject. Promote a finding only when it supports a prompt-addressable editorial decision or reveals a repeated misunderstanding, correction, trust problem, or successful communication pattern.

Comment likes show visibility, not correctness. Comment volume does not replace the primary KPI unless qualified conversation is the stated objective.

## Route learnings

- Selection, routing, sources, or evidence availability → `research.md`.
- Hook, information order, wording, attribution, or CTA → `caption.md`.
- Source image, composition, visual evidence, privacy, or image production → `image.md`.
- Page-authored first comment or reply behavior → `engagement.md`.
- Stable visual identity or layout → `design.md`.

Prefer the smallest additive instruction. When a finding conflicts with an existing rule, show the exact old and proposed wording and preserve everything unrelated.
